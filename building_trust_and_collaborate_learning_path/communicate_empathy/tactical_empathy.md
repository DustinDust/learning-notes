### 1. Recognize your communication style

How we speak to people affects how they respond. First step toward empathetic communication is honestly understanding my default style.

Some common styles:

- assertive and direct: get to point quickly with few sidetracks
- aggressive: dominate conversation and make sure own opinion is heard
- passive: go with flow and let other person lead
- passive aggressive: express feeling indirectly through sarcasm, procrastination or subtle behavior

May also be animated and emotional, or more reflective and measured. Need to identify where I am now and where I want to be.

Self assessment isnt enough because how I THINK I sound may be different from how people experience me. Managers sometimes learn this through 360 reviews, where direct reports show how their communication actually lands.

Can conduct a smaller communication 360 by asking a few coworkers individually, preferably people I'm not too close with. Explain that honest feedback will help improve communication and ask about 2 types of situation:

1. Structured communication such as meetings and presentations
2. Free flowing communication such as one on ones and casual chats

### 2. Avoid stumbling blocks of empathetic communication

Our thoughts and feelings about communication shape how we behave. Before improving empathy, need to examine what happens in my mind.

Questions to ask:

- Does empathy feel helpful, hopeful, intimidating or scary?
- Does listening make me feel empowered, uneasy or burdened?
- Why is communication challenging for me?
- Where did negative story about my communication come from?
- How can I rewrite it and regain confidence?

The WHY questions lead toward HOW to change behavior. Thoughts regularly show up through body language, even when words sound correct. Course mention that up to 93% of communication is nonverbal.

Things to notice:

- posture and whether I'm facing person
- leaning in vs slouching or crossing arms
- fast, labored or measured breathing
- tension in face and jaw
- whether facial expression matches interest in conversation

Approach conversation with confidence and intention to form or strengthen connection. This helps both people express their point instead of one side only.

=> Address mind to control body. If I enter with resentment or fear, it will probably leak out somewhere.

### 3. Foresee when empathy is challenging and how to plan ahead

Believing empathy is impossible can become self fulfilling. Need to learn how I typically react with different people and situations.

Examples of patterns:

- one person always triggers impatience, so I rush them
- another makes me uncomfortable, so I become apathetic and just force conversation through
- certain topic makes me focus on arguing instead of understanding

Once I know my pattern, I can plan a different behavior.

**Personal Notes**

- empathy isnt equally difficult with everyone, pretending otherwise hides useful pattern
- good idea to study easy conversations too. Improvement isnt only about staring at failures
- planning ahead could be as simple as deciding not to interrupt certain person or allowing extra time
- apathetic communication may look calm from outside even though I'm mentally checked out

### 4. Find empathetic communication mentors

Mentor doesnt need to be senior leader who already achieved exact career I want. Guidance can come from people who regularly observe my communication.

3 places to learn:

a. Trusted people in personal life

Choose 1 or 2 people who see how I talk in different context and can be honest without damaging relationship. They may notice when I'm strong and when conversation goes off rail.

b. Peers at work

Coworkers at similar level understand job, audience and daily challenges. Ask 1 or 2 trusted peers to say something when they notice useful pattern. Their criticism can be more relevant because they know the actual work.

c. Wider learning

Read communication experts, but also explore psychology, marketing and conversation heavy fiction. Empathy involves critical thinking, understanding and openness to narratives different from my own, not only speaking and listening techniques.

=> Build community of people who help improve communication. Everyone can be mentor if I'm willing to observe, ask and learn.
