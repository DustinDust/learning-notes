### 1. Three essential skills for collaborative leadership

Command and control isnt necessarily wrong, but author argues it has become irrelevant in modern networked workplace. Collaborative leaders need 3 key skills:

a. Empathetic listening

Goal is to understand speaker's perspective. Focus completely instead of preparing response or thinking about other thing.

Hardest part for many leader is resisting urge to solve problem too early. Sometimes person only wants sounding board. Even when advice is wanted, listening first gives more complete information.

b. Social sensitivity

Google's study of successful teams found 2 shared behaviors:

- members spoke roughly equal amount, with nobody dominating or disappearing into silence
- members were good at noticing feelings through body language, tone and facial expression

This ability to read emotional cues is social sensitivity. Course suggests "Reading the Mind in the Eyes" test as team exercise.

c. Visual thinking

Use simple drawing to explain concept. One activity is putting people in group of 4 or 5, asking each group to define collaboration and represent it on large paper. Artistic skill doesnt matter, conversation created by explaining drawing is the point.

=> Start with empathetic listening because it can be practiced in every interaction.

**Personal Notes**

- common theme again: dont solve before fully understanding what person needs
- equal speaking time doesnt mean forcing exact timer, more like noticing domination and silence
- visual thinking might expose different definitions faster than another abstract discussion
- command and control still has place in emergency IMO, but shouldnt be default for normal work

### 2. Collaborative body language

Confidence and assertiveness show leadership presence, but collaborative leader also need warmth and empathy. Goal is making people feel valued, respected and included.

7 nonverbal signals:

1. Make eye contact
1. Smile
1. Use head signals
1. Keep body open
1. Lean in
1. Face speaker with shoulders
1. Synchronize body language

=> Power signals help project authority, while warm signals invite collaboration. Leader need both depending on moment.

**Personal Notes**

- overlaps a lot with empathy and effective listening courses, which probably means these signals actually matter
- eye contact advice need cultural context, since it isnt interpreted same everywhere
- mirroring should stay natural. Doing it deliberately too much would feel creepy lol
- body language should match actual attention, gestures alone cant hide that I'm mentally elsewhere

### 3. Brainstorming to increase collaboration

Inclusive brainstorming helps prevent 1 or 2 loud people from controlling discussion while quieter team member contribute nothing.

Basic process:

1. Warm up: Use small word game, puzzle or humor if group isnt used to brainstorming. Goal is relaxed and playful atmosphere.
1. Generate ideas: Focus on quantity before quality. Encourage any idea, including obvious or silly ones, and allow people to build on each other's input. No judging or criticism during this phase. Near end, try "get fired" technique: ask for ideas that could work but are so outrageous they might get group fired. Then tone them down into practical option.
1. Evaluate ideas: Now introduce constructive conflict. Ask people to critique solutions from view of other departments, management levels and customers affected.

Ways to handle common issues:

a. Use LCS model for criticism

- Like: what I like about idea
- Concern: what worries me
- Solve: how we might address concern

b. If one person dominates, split into groups of 3 or 4

c. Make negativity visible during idea generation, such as giving a silly object to whoever makes negative comment

d. If quieter people are overshadowed, let everyone write ideas on sticky notes instead of speaking immediately

=> Brainstorming supports creativity, equal contribution and practice with constructive conflict.

**Personal Notes**

- LCS is better than vague "be constructive" rule because it gives exact format
- This one is prob one of the most direct, straightforward section in the entire path with clear signals, path and process. Very nice
